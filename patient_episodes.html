Pre-Examination Checklist
Complete before physical examination
Have you washed your hands?
If you are examining a female patient, is there a female chaperon in the room?
Have you greeted the patient by name?
Ask the patient to sit/lie down comfortably, assist if necessary.
Watch as the patient walks to the chair/couch.
Ask the patient if there is any pain anywhere, and if so, approach that region last and very slowly.
Confirm patient identity (name and ID) before proceeding.
